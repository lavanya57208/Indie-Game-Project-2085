## 🎮 THE WORLD OF 2085

> **The future was never meant to be stable.**

The year is **2085**.

Humanity has pushed technology beyond its limits.  
Cities have become machines. Energy systems have become unstable.  
And somewhere inside the chaos, a mysterious phenomenon has begun to distort reality.

You are a runner trapped inside this collapsing system.

There is no finish line.

There is no safe path.

There is only one question:

# **HOW LONG CAN YOU SURVIVE?**

---

# 🏃 GAMEPLAY

**PROJECT AQUA 2085** is a fast-paced 3D infinite runner where the player must continuously move forward, react to incoming obstacles, collect energy crystals, and survive for as long as possible.

The longer you survive, the faster the world becomes.

### Your objective:

```text
        RUN
         ↓
      REACT
         ↓
      DODGE
         ↓
     COLLECT
         ↓
      SURVIVE
         ↓
      GO FASTER
         ↓
      SURVIVE
         ↺
